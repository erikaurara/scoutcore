About IXMetrics

IXMetrics turns current MLB data into clear, explainable research so fans and analysts can understand what matters without sorting through dozens of disconnected statistics.

AI Game Intelligence Transparent methodology Regularly updated data

THE PURPOSE

Make Baseball Data Easier to Understand

Baseball provides an enormous amount of information. IXMetrics organizes schedule details, recent player performance, team context, probable pitchers, and pitching or bullpen trends into focused research views.

The goal is not to replace your judgment. It is to show the evidence behind each signal or comparison so you can understand why it appeared and decide what deserves a closer look.

OUR METHODOLOGY

From Raw Data to Explainable Context

01

Collect

IXMetrics reads current schedule, game, team, player, and probable-pitcher information from MLB data services.

02

Organize

The product turns separate data points into consistent game, player, matchup, and team-comparison views.

03

Check rules

Automated rules look for qualifying recent form and pitching or bullpen trends using available verified data.

04

Explain

Published signals include the supporting statistic and a plain-language reason instead of an unexplained label.

AI GAME INTELLIGENCE

What “AI” Means at IXMetrics

AI Game Intelligence refers to the automated system used to collect, compare, filter, and explain baseball data. It helps IXMetrics update consistently as new information becomes available.
