EMAIL SUPPORT

How can we help?

Email IXMetrics Support and include enough detail for us to understand the issue. Never email your password, verification code, or payment details.

support.ixmetrics@gmail.comOpen email →

Helpful information to include

  • The page or feature involved
  • What you expected to happen
  • What happened instead
  • Your device and browser
  • A screenshot with private details hidden

SAFETY

Protect your account

1 IXMetrics Support will never ask for your password or verification code.

2 Do not email payment-card details, government identification, or private documents unless specifically required through a secure process.

3 Hide personal information belonging to other people before attaching screenshots.

4 For community safety concerns, use the in-product report tool when available and include the relevant post or profile.
