DAILY INTELLIGENCE FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is IXMetrics Daily Intelligence?

It is an automatically updated summary of MLB player form, pitcher trends, bullpen watch items, and available matchup context that clears IXMetrics data rules.

How are daily signals selected?

IXMetrics checks verified recent game logs and available matchup data against explainable rules. A signal appears only when enough supporting information is available.

Do signals guarantee a game or player outcome?

No. Signals are baseball research clues based on available data and never guarantee an outcome.
